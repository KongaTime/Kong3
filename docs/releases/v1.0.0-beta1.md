# Kong³ v1.0.0-beta1

RPCS3, the PlayStation 3 emulator, as a homebrew title for the PS5 (`PPSA99200`),
renamed Kong³: the first release you can download.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Piracy is not condoned.** This release contains no games, no BIOS files, no
> console firmware and no decryption keys, and none will ever be provided or linked
> to. Use only **legally obtained backups of games you own**, made yourself from your
> own discs or digital purchases, and the PS3 system software from Sony's own
> `PS3UPDAT.PUP`. Please don't ask for, or post links to, games, firmware or keys in
> issues or discussions: they will be removed.

> [!NOTE]
> **The built title is attached, and you may share it.** v0.1.0-beta.1 was source only:
> RPCS3 is GPL-2.0-only, and the PS5 platform layer every title linked was
> GPL-3.0-or-later, which cannot be combined in a program that is shared. That layer
> is now this title's own, MIT, and nothing in the download is GPL-3.0-or-later. As a
> whole it is GPL-2.0-only, RPCS3's licence; its complete source is in
> [v1.0.0-beta1: source](https://github.com/KongaTime/PS5_RPCS3Title/releases/tag/v1.0.0-beta1-source),
> and `licenses/README.txt` in the title lists every part, its licence and its source.

## Highlights

- **A download.** The PS5 libre layer (`ps5/libre/`, MIT) replaces the GPL-3.0 parts
  every title linked: the start-up, the memory layout and `libc.prx`; the heap,
  virtual memory and threads; and the C library functions the console does not give
  a title (files, directories, networking, regular expressions on musl's engine,
  locales, kernel log output): some 170 functions in all. Each part was written from the standards and
  the console's behaviour, and proven on the console before the next.
- **Checked at every build.** `ps5/tools/shareable.py` reads what the linker took and
  names anything GPL-3.0 in the title; the release refuses one that has any. It also
  checks that the tool packaging `eboot.bin` adds no code of its own (it adds none:
  measured byte for byte).
- **Kong³**: a new name, logo and home-screen background. The title id is the same,
  so your settings, saves and games carry over.
- **Frame generation**: the title's own, as before. Lossless Scaling's and
  MihawkGen's are GPL-3.0-or-later, so they are not in the download (see below).

## What is not in the download

- **Lossless Scaling's and MihawkGen's frame generation.** Their settings are hidden;
  the title's own frame generation is the only one.
- **RPCN (online play).** Its TLS library, wolfSSL, is GPL-3.0-or-later. RPCN stops
  at a wolfSSL error and stays offline.

Both are still in the source: a build of your own with `PS5_GPL3_FRAME_GENERATION=1`
or `PS5_RPCN=1` (`rpcs3/build-rpcs3.sh`) has them, and has GPL-3.0 code in it: keep
such builds to yourself.

## Known issues

- Heavy games run well below full speed: the emulated SPUs are the limit on the
  console's CPU. Frame generation smooths motion; it does not speed the game up.
- Fast camera turns with frame generation can smear or double edges.
- A game's first boot compiles for minutes with little on screen; new areas stutter
  while their shaders compile the first time.
- Some games freeze unless **Accurate SPU Reservations** is on (the default).
- **The home screen keeps the old name and picture** after updating from
  v0.1.0-beta.1: ShadowMountPlus copied them when it registered the title. Delete the
  tile and let it register the title again; your data in
  `/data/homebrew/PPSA99200/` stays.

## Install

1. Unpack the ZIP and copy `PPSA99200/` to the console's `/data/homebrew/PPSA99200/`
   over FTP, then register it with ShadowMountPlus.
2. Put Sony's `PS3UPDAT.PUP` in `/data/homebrew/PPSA99200/` and launch the title: it
   installs the PS3 system software once.
3. Put your own game backups in `/data/homebrew/PPSA99200/rpcs3/games/`.

Updating from v0.1.0-beta.1: copy the new folder over the old one; your settings,
saves, games and caches stay. Building it yourself:
[`rpcs3/README.md`](https://github.com/KongaTime/PS5_RPCS3Title/blob/v1.0.0-beta1/rpcs3/README.md).

## Reporting problems

Send `rpcs3/cache/RPCS3.log` and `rpcs3-trace.txt` from the title's folder, copied
before you launch again, and say what you saw and when. Never attach games,
firmware, keys or saves.
